Vaibhav Peddakadapa

Vaibhav is a Year 2 Computer Engineering undergraduate at Nanyang Technological University, specialising in AI. He is most at home in full stack development, and is drawn to problems where technology meets finance: trading systems, pricing engines and data driven decision making. He holds the NTU-Google Career Certificate Scholarship and the AWS AI & ML Scholarship, and also writes in his spare time.

He was a Full Stack Developer Intern at Tata Consultancy Services, where he became the first person from Singapore to complete an overseas internship with the company under the Global Ready Talent Programme, working in the Singapore Airlines Offshore Delivery Centre. Before university, he served with the 1st Battalion Singapore Guards, where his platoon was named Best Platoon in Battalion.
